Self-Powered Intelligent Flexible Electronics

Electronics that power themselves, sense intelligently, and work in the air, in space and under the sea.

Welcome to the Lionel Liang Lab

We are an interdisciplinary team at the School of Flexible Electronics, Northwestern Polytechnical University (NWPU) working at the interface of materials chemistry, energy harvesting and flexible electronics. Our goal is to make electronics that power themselves — harvesting energy from motion, water droplets, moisture and the human body — and to deploy them in health electronics, wearable sensing, and special equipment for aerospace and marine applications.

Our research is organised around one theme — Self-Powered Intelligent Flexible Electronics — and three pillars: self-powered energy-harvesting materials and devices, intelligent AI-driven sensing materials and systems, and flexible electronics for extreme environments in aeronautics, astronautics and marine engineering.

We collaborate closely with The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, Nanyang Technological University (Singapore), and other leading institutions.

Research Pillars

Self-Powered ↗

Pillar 01 · Energy-harvesting materials & device design

We design materials and devices that generate their own electricity from motion, water and moisture — so that flexible electronics can run without batteries.

Research

Intelligent ↗

Pillar 02 · AI-based sensing materials & AI-empowered sensing systems

We bring artificial intelligence into both ends of the sensing chain: using data-driven methods to discover and design better sensing materials, and building smart, self-powered systems that understand the signals they collect.

Research

Flexible for Extreme Environments ↗

Pillar 03 · Flexible electronics for aeronautics, astronautics & marine engineering

We take flexible, self-powered electronics out of the lab and into the air, space and sea — conformal sensors that keep working on aircraft surfaces, in orbit and underwater.

A Note from the PI

Welcome, and thank you for visiting!

I started this lab with a simple belief: good science grows best in a place where people feel supported, curious, and free to try new ideas. Our work on self-powered electronics — devices that draw energy from motion, water and the human body — is exciting, but what I value most is the team behind it.

In our group, every member is a colleague, not just a pair of hands. I meet with each student regularly, I try to help each person find a project that matches their interests, and I care about your growth as a scientist and as a person — whether your path leads to academia, industry, or somewhere new.

If you share our curiosity about energy, materials and electronics that can make a difference in health and engineering, I would be glad to hear from you. Our door is always open.

— Fei Liang, Tenured Associate Professor, Ph.D. Supervisor, Principal Investigator (PI) of the Self-Powered Intelligent Flexible Sensing Group
