Join Us

We are recruiting highly self-motivated Ph.D. students and postdoctoral researchers — and we would love to meet you.

You will not be doing this alone

Starting a research career can feel daunting. In our lab, we want every new member to feel welcome from day one. We believe that people do their best science when they feel supported, respected and free to ask questions — any question.

How we mentor

🤝

Regular one-on-one time

Each member meets with the PI regularly to talk through results, plans and anything else on their mind.

🌱

Projects that fit you

We help you find a project that matches your interests and background, and grow your independence step by step.

🧭

Your career, your path

Academia, industry, or something new — we support your goals with writing, presentation and career guidance.

💬

A kind, open team

Senior members help newcomers, mistakes are treated as part of learning, and everyone's voice is heard in group meetings.

What we offer

  • A well-funded research environment with active national and industry-supported projects.
  • International collaboration with The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, Nanyang Technological University (Singapore) and other renowned institutions.
  • Opportunities to study abroad — several of our Master’s and Ph.D. students have joined partner groups for joint training through the university’s China Scholarship Council (CSC) exchange programs.
  • Meaningful problems — advancing self-powered flexible electronics toward new applications in health care and in special equipment for aerospace and marine environments.

Open positions

🎓

Ph.D. students

Join us to work on self-powered, intelligent and flexible electronics, with close mentoring and opportunities for joint training abroad.

🔬

Postdoctoral researchers

Lead your own project within one of our three research pillars, mentor students, and build an independent research profile.

Backgrounds we are looking for

  • Triboelectric nanogenerators (TENG) and energy-harvesting devices
  • Electronic circuits — power management, signal acquisition, wireless and embedded systems
  • Artificial intelligence — machine learning for materials design or sensor-signal recognition
  • Sensing materials — flexible, stretchable and functional materials for sensors

What matters most

Above all, we look for people with strong self-drive: curious, hard-working, and eager to take ownership of their research. You don’t need to know everything already — motivation and willingness to learn matter most, and we will support you along the way.

Say hello

Send an email to iamfliang@nwpu.edu.cn with your CV, transcripts (for Ph.D. applicants) or a publication list (for postdocs), and a few lines about what excites you in research. Please use the subject line “Application – [Ph.D./Postdoc] – Your Name”. Informal questions are very welcome too — we are happy to chat before you apply.
