We are an interdisciplinary team at the **School of Flexible Electronics, Northwestern Polytechnical University (NWPU)** working at the interface of materials chemistry, energy harvesting and flexible electronics. Our goal is to make electronics that **power themselves** — harvesting energy from motion, water droplets, moisture and the human body — and to deploy them in **health electronics**, **wearable sensing**, and **special equipment for aerospace and marine applications**.

Our research is organised around one theme — **Self-Powered Intelligent Flexible Electronics** — and three pillars: **self-powered** energy-harvesting materials and devices, **intelligent** AI-driven sensing materials and systems, and **flexible electronics for extreme environments** in aeronautics, astronautics and marine engineering.

We collaborate closely with The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, Nanyang Technological University (Singapore), and other leading institutions.
