### Self-Powered Intelligent Flexible Electronics

Our goal is electronics that **power themselves**, **think for themselves**, and **work where conventional electronics cannot**. We build this from three connected pillars — energy-harvesting materials and devices, AI-driven sensing, and flexible systems for aeronautics, astronautics and marine engineering — each strengthening the others.
