## Open positions

<div class="values">
<div class="value card"><span class="vi">🎓</span><h4>Ph.D. students</h4><p>Join us to work on self-powered, intelligent and flexible electronics, with close mentoring and opportunities for joint training abroad.</p></div>
<div class="value card"><span class="vi">🔬</span><h4>Postdoctoral researchers</h4><p>Lead your own project within one of our three research pillars, mentor students, and build an independent research profile.</p></div>
</div>

### Backgrounds we are looking for

- **Triboelectric nanogenerators (TENG)** and energy-harvesting devices
- **Electronic circuits** — power management, signal acquisition, wireless and embedded systems
- **Artificial intelligence** — machine learning for materials design or sensor-signal recognition
- **Sensing materials** — flexible, stretchable and functional materials for sensors

### What matters most

Above all, we look for people with **strong self-drive**: curious, hard-working, and eager to take ownership of their research. You don't need to know everything already — motivation and willingness to learn matter most, and we will support you along the way.

## How to apply

<div class="hello card">

Send an email to **[iamfliang@nwpu.edu.cn](mailto:iamfliang@nwpu.edu.cn)** with your CV, transcripts (for Ph.D. applicants) or a publication list (for postdocs), and a few lines about what excites you in research. Please use the subject line *“Application – [Ph.D./Postdoc] – Your Name”*. Informal questions are very welcome too — we are happy to chat before you apply.
